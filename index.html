02 / BEHIND THE SCENES

FROM IDEA
TO ANIMATION.

This project started as an exploration of how a song could be translated into a visual world through 3D animation.

The project became an opportunity to experiment with modeling, animation, lighting, composition, and storytelling.

This page documents some of the process, experiments, and visual development behind the finished video.
